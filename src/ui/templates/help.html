TRANSPORT · GUIDE

What you can do

Views

  • Locations — the icon dock at bottom left jumps between curated displays (NSW, VIC, Sydney, Melbourne, Newcastle). The menu button lists every display.
  • Missions — the first-run launcher offers Live contacts, Space missions, and Environmental (NSW power, ports, mines, earthquakes, fires). Reopen it any time from a fresh tab.
  • Map stacks — Visual presets switch the basemap between Esri satellite, OSM, and Bing (Bing needs a Cesium ion key in Provider settings).
  • Shortcuts — the button row under TRANSPORT toggles LOCATION, DATA LAYERS and SCENES; the top-center apps button holds globe actions (clear layers, share, tilt, north-up, reset).

Layers

  • DATA LAYERS (left) lists everything toggleable: aircraft, vessels, earthquakes, fires, Australian power plants, ports and mines, CCTV cameras, radio stations, satellites, dams, datacenters, submarine cables and more.
  • Enabled layers draw on the globe immediately; the bottom bar counts what is showing.
  • CLEAR / the layers-clear globe button turns every data layer off at once.

Ask the map

  • Voice — press the mic in Agent Chat (or the dock mic) to talk: try “fly to Sydney”, “show power plants”, “switch to OSM”, “next CCTV camera”, “track that aircraft”, “annotate this substation”.
  • Chat — type in Agent Chat whenever voice is off; it rides the same agent turn.
  • The agent can fly the camera, toggle layers and panels, switch styles and stacks, drive CCTV, radio and the cockpit, annotate, track contacts, and look up satellite passes.

Imagery and cameras

  • CCTV / IMAGES (right) plays a random slideshow of live area cameras. Pick any camera to hold it; AUTO HOP resumes the slideshow.
  • RECENT IMAGERY — draw a box to list the last 30 days of satellite passes, pin days, and swipe before/after with the divider.

Share and keys

  • Copy share link (top bar, menu, or globe button) copies a link that restores this exact view, layers and panels.
  • Provider settings (POWER UP chip, bottom right) adds keys for voice, live ships, fires, traffic and more. The globe flies keyless without them.
  • ESC closes dialogs and cancels tools; C exits the cockpit.
